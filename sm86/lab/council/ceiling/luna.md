CEILING: **Theoretical ~560 tok/s; practical ~215 tok/s** (rough practical range 205–225). The theoretical figure is an optimistic full-window roofline, not a forecast:

- HC reads add `97 × 13.1 MB` = **1.27 GB/window**. Native projections add 2,069 MiB = **2.17 GB**; the non-HC part of the 1,466 MiB pack adds about 0.27 GB. That is about **9.93 GB** from GDDR6X. At 936 GB/s, the transfer bound is **10.6 ms**.

This assumes peak bandwidth, perfect eight-token acceptance, and no extra kernel, CPU-tail, or synchronization cost. At the measured window shape instead—T≈3.9 and 3.24 kept tokens—the same bandwidth arithmetic gives a resource-only ceiling around **370 tok/s**. The practical estimate is lower because of exact-kernel efficiency and the layer-by-layer dependency chain. Today’s 168 tok/s is **30% of the ideal full-window roofline and 78% of the practical estimate**; it is about 45% of the current-window-shape roofline.

PREFILL: **Cold 64K: 21.0 s now; estimated full-path floor ~8–12 s. Warm turn: 842 ms now; estimated practical floor ~650–770 ms.**

- Cold measurements are 1.8–2.1 s at 4K, about 5.5–5.7 s at 16K, 10.9–11.1 s at 32K, and 21.0 s at 64K. The 64K prompt uses two 32K chunks. If nearly all 39.97 GiB of experts are streamed for each chunk, transfer alone is `2 × 39.97 GiB / 26.8 GB/s ≈ 3.2 s`. That is a transfer lower bound, not a full prefill floor.
- As a compute sanity check, at roughly 3.1 bits/weight, a 1.66 MiB expert is about 4.3 million weights. Each prompt token routes to 480 experts, or roughly 4.1 GFLOP/token for multiply-adds. A 64K prompt is about 270 TFLOP of routed-expert work. At 35.6 TFLOP/s FP32 peak, that is **7.6 s expert-only**; GDN, QSA, projections, and real quantized-kernel inefficiency raise the full-path floor. The 8–12 s estimate is an inference, not a measured bound.
- The warm fit is `505 ms fixed + 0.404 ms/new token`. The median implies about `(842−505)/0.404 ≈ 834` new tokens. At the measured long-prompt rate of about 3,120 tok/s, those new tokens take at least ~267 ms, giving **~772 ms** if the fixed term stays. The fitted fixed term is not shown to be irreducible. The code has a conversation snapshot restore path ([generate.cpp](/C:/src/Strata/src/program/generate.cpp:16210)); its contribution to the 505 ms has not been separated.
